## Reading dates and status

The current-state notes are a checkpoint dated **23 October 2026**. Historical reviews retain their original findings, counts and test scope. Follow their repair links before treating an old finding as an open defect. A recorded test result is evidence for that test run, rather than a fresh verification of the entire application.

Source code, SQL patches, backups and release archives remain in their repository locations. The catalog records their paths and the provenance of imported notes. The PDF manual is included inside this vault for offline reading.
