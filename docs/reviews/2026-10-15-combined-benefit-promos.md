# Senior/PWD with automatic product promos — 15 October 2026

Choose **Senior Citizen** or **PWD** in POS, enter the beneficiary name/ID, confirm the documents and select eligible products. Automatic product promotions remain available alongside the selected benefit.

Updated on 16 October: category offers now apply automatically too and are removed from the dropdown. See [the current category promotion guide](2026-10-16-automatic-category-promos.md) for allocation rules, validation and the current hosting ZIP. The verification figures below describe the earlier product-only implementation.

Individual product/category promotions and the Automatic product promos option are no longer listed in the Discount dropdown. Their rules are supplied separately to the automatic calculation. **No Discount** is the default and means no additional manual or beneficiary discount; matching product/category promos still apply. Senior Citizen, PWD, and available manual offers for all products remain selectable. A saved selection of a removed offer or the previous empty No Discount choice falls back to the new No Discount default. The internal `auto` value is retained for checkout compatibility.

Every qualifying product now receives its own best product promo, including when the dropdown changes to Senior/PWD. Several different product promos can apply in the same sale. Multiple offers on one product use the highest saving, then the lowest discount ID for ties. Product minimums/caps apply only to matching items. Each overlapping item uses either its promo or its statutory treatment, including applicable VAT relief, whichever lowers the payable amount. Equal savings retain statutory treatment. Remaining statutory centavos are recalculated among the lines that keep that treatment. Promos stay visible while beneficiary fields are incomplete; checkout remains blocked until verification and product selection are complete.

Example: a PHP 55 product with a 20% promo receives PHP 11 off. A separate selected PHP 112 VAT-inclusive medicine receives PHP 12 VAT relief and PHP 20 Senior/PWD discount. Total payable: **PHP 124**. For an overlapping PHP 112 item, a 25% promo yields PHP 84 payable, while statutory treatment yields PHP 80, so statutory wins. A 30% promo yields PHP 78.40 and wins instead; that line keeps its ordinary tax treatment and receives no statutory VAT relief.

The calculation follows the choice between promotional and statutory treatment described in [DOH AO 2010-0032 for senior medicine purchases](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/57534) and [DOH AO 2017-0008, section VI.3(e)-(f), for PWD purchases](https://ncda.gov.ph/disability-laws/administrative-orders/doh-ao-2017-0008-implementing-guidelines-of-republic-act-10754-otherwise-known-as-an-act-expanding-the-benefits-and-privileges-of-persons-with-disability-for/). Product eligibility and beneficiary documents still require the existing staff verification.

For example, add another PHP 100 product with a PHP 15 fixed promo to the PHP 124 mixed-benefit example: both promos remain, and the new total is **PHP 209**. If the PHP 112 item instead has a winning 40% promo, all three promos apply and total payable is **PHP 196.20**, without statutory VAT relief on the promo-priced line.

The server validates every displayed promo ID, product and centavo amount, then rechecks every applied rule under deterministic checkout locks. Per-item discounts, VAT relief and statutory classification are retained. Both components are saved in `sales.receipt_metadata`; existing sale item fields hold actual applied amounts. Multiple-promo sales have a null legacy `discount_id`; the full rule list and names are in the immutable metadata rather than pointing the combined amount at a single coupon. Single automatic promos retain their linked ID. Completed statutory and automatic-promo invoices require a refund and new POS sale for quantity changes; notes/payment corrections remain available. Refunds use saved net item values. Older receipts retain their original amounts and presentation.
