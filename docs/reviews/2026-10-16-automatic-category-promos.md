# Automatic product and category promotions — 16 October 2026

POS defaults to **No Discount**, which means no additional manual or beneficiary discount. Active product and category promotions still apply automatically. Individual product/category offers and the automatic-promos option are hidden from the dropdown. **Senior Citizen**, **PWD**, and manual all-product offers remain available. Saved choices for a removed category offer return to No Discount.

Each item receives its best product promo, allocated category promo, or selected Senior/PWD treatment. These treatments do not stack on the same item. Comparison includes statutory VAT relief; equal savings retain statutory treatment. Equal ordinary offers use the lower discount ID. Ordinary promo lines keep their normal tax treatment. Beneficiary documents and eligible products must still be confirmed before checkout.

A category promo uses only the original gross subtotals of matching category items to test its minimum spend. Its fixed discount or percentage cap applies once to that category in the transaction. The resulting amount is shared proportionally across matching lines, with exact centavos allocated by largest remainder and product ID order. Each line then compares that share against its other offers. Shares replaced by a better product offer or statutory treatment are not redistributed. This keeps category fixed amounts and caps from being multiplied across products.

Example: two matching items priced PHP 100 and PHP 50 share a fixed PHP 15 category promo as PHP 10 and PHP 5. An unrelated-category item gets none. For a PHP 112 VAT-inclusive medicine and PHP 55 matching item with a 25% category promo, a verified Senior/PWD buyer pays PHP 80 on the selected medicine and PHP 41.25 on the other item. Adding an unrelated PHP 100 item gives a total of **PHP 221.25**. Without a beneficiary discount, the same three items total **PHP 225.25**.

Checkout validates every displayed promo and item amount, rechecks each applied rule once, and locks the relevant category records. Deleted categories, changed rules and product recategorization cannot silently complete with an obsolete quote. Existing receipt metadata saves rule scope, category, product and applied amount; sale item records save actual discounts and VAT relief. Receipts and Sale Details show **Promotions** alongside statutory deductions. Refunds use the saved net item amounts. Quantity changes on completed automatic-promo invoices require a refund and a new POS sale; notes/payment corrections remain available.
